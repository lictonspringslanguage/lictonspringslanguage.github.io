Privacy policy

Licton Springs Language collects no data at all. Here are the details.

Effective October 26, 2026

Licton Springs Language is a reading and writing adventure for children. It is built to work entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal information, from children or anyone else, because none of it ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or data-collecting SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Progress and settings

Your child's story progress, answers, skill levels, word notebook and stickers, and the settings a grown-up chooses in the parent area (grade, optional test scores used as starting levels, strand focus, daily time limit, reading options), are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it unless you export a save file yourself.

Save files

In the parent area, a grown-up can export a save file to move progress to another device. The file goes only where you send it (for example AirDrop, Files or email), and the app never sees where that is. Importing reads only the file you pick.

Voices

Story lines and lessons are recordings that ship inside the app. Some puzzle instructions and hints are read by your device's built-in speech voice, which Licton Springs Language uses without recording or sending anything.

Purchases

If a grown-up buys the full story, the App Store or Google Play handles the payment. Licton Springs Language only learns whether the unlock was bought; it never sees your name or payment details. Purchases sit behind a grown-ups check.

Permissions

None. Licton Springs Language doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or internet.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. This is true for children under 13 (COPPA), and in every country.

Deleting your data

Deleting Licton Springs Language from your device removes all of its data. A grown-up can also reset progress in the parent area. Save files you exported stay wherever you saved them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
